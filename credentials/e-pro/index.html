e-PRO

e-PRO

Mostly decorative

Who issues it

National Association of Realtors

What it teaches

Technology and online marketing for agents.

What an agent has to do to get it

Two online courses and a fee.

What it actually tells you

This one ages fast. The technology in the curriculum is not the technology that sells your house this year. Look at the actual marketing they produced for their last three listings instead.

Ask this instead

Pull up your last three listings on your phone. Show me the photos, the video, and the first page of Google for that address.

Where I stand on it

I do not hold these letters. I studied the material behind a lot of them, because the subjects are real and a seller pays for what I do not know. I never bought the initials. If a credential is ever shown to get sellers more money, I will earn it that day and put it on this page myself.
